# Copywriting Skill — High-Converting Web & Product Copy

> Contrato de copywriting para el sitio marketing de Puff Cross Media (Design-as-a-Service).

## Core Principles

1. **Outcome > Features**: Never sell "design services" or "unlimited requests"; sell speed, scale, brand prestige, and frictionless execution.
2. **Clear > Clever**: Avoid vague marketing jargon. Be direct, punchy, and instantly understandable within 3 seconds of scanning.
3. **Active Voice & Sentence Case**: Use active verbs (`Get design on demand`, `Scale your collateral`, `Ship faster`). Use sentence case for body text and punchy uppercase for display titles.
4. **Friction Reduction**: CTAs and microcopy must answer "What happens next?" and eliminate hesitation.

---

## Landing Page Frameworks

### 1. Hero Section Architecture
- **Headline (H1)**: High-impact value promise (e.g. *"Design On Demand for Fast-Growing Brands"*).
- **Subheadline**: Expands on how it works in 1-2 tight sentences (e.g. *"Pause or cancel anytime. Get high-converting packaging, collateral, and digital assets delivered in days."*).
- **Primary CTA**: Low-friction action verb + benefit (e.g. *"See Plans & Pricing"*, *"Book a 15-Min Intro"*).
- **Social Proof / Micro-badge**: Reinforces trust (e.g. *"Trusted by 40+ brands worldwide"*).

### 2. Feature & Service Sections (PAS & AIDA)
- **Problem**: Identify the client pain point (e.g. *"In-house hiring is slow. Freelancers are unreliable."*).
- **Agitate**: Explain the cost of inaction (e.g. *"Wasting weeks on recruitment and managing inconsistent design quality."*).
- **Solution**: Present Puff Cross Media as the streamlined alternative (e.g. *"One flat monthly subscription. World-class design pieces delivered continuously."*).

### 3. Case Study & Portfolio Microcopy
- **Title**: Action or deliverable focused (e.g. *"Epa Burguer Packaging & Collateral"*).
- **Summary**: 1 sentence summarizing the deliverable scope.
- **Challenge / Solution / Outcome**: Tight bullet points highlighting real execution, not generic buzzwords.

## Copy Checklist

- [ ] Is the headline readable in under 3 seconds?
- [ ] Does the copy focus on client outcomes instead of internal agency processes?
- [ ] Are all CTAs active, specific, and friction-free?
- [ ] Is the language in English (site copy contract) with correct sentence case?
